Illustrator & Graphic Designer

Born in Tuscany, built in Venice.

I graduated in Product & Visual Design from Iuav in 2024 and am currently in my second year of a Master’s degree in Graphic Design.

My goal is to translate a concept into a cohesive visual form listening to the needs of clients and collaborators while always infusing the project with personality.

I have six years of experience in the hospitality industry and spent a month working at a vintage shop in London; since 2025, I have been working as a freelancer, taking on commissions for posters, logos, and visual identities.

Maestria Pipistrelli — Exiting the Vampire Castle EP

An electro EP by a duo connected across a trajectory that runs from the cold salt of the Baltic Sea, through a river moving south, until it slows and settles into a lagoon. A shared impulse to exit the vampire castle and reclaim something taken. The castle is a shifting maze of mirrors, roles, and borrowed identities. Moving through it means stepping away from moral posturing and abandoning performance. Only beyond its walls does a different kind of energy begin to circulate. Released January 16, 2026. Available on Soundcloud and Bandcamp.

Poster Design · Cheapfestival · 2025

Liminal Landscape

The poster for the Cheap Festival’s "LIMINAL LANDSCAPES" contest features a "non-place" as its subject: the landscape of a small house perfectly centered between sky and earth—an image used to test the focus of the eye's depth receptors. It is a non-place that has never been visited, yet exists in the childhood memories of many.

Contest: @cheapfestival

Poster Design · CHEAP contest · 2026

FIGHT THE DISREGARD

The poster for the Cheap Festival 2026 Contest, on the theme "Conflict and Care", is born from the collaboration between Matteo Castelli and Angelo Cimino. The project combines Cimino's photographs taken in Marghera (Ve), reworked using Photoshop without relying on artificial intelligence. The visual composition creates a strong contrast between the messages of Pro-Palestine propaganda and the police intervention to block the demonstration. The poster explicitly aims to highlight the raw visual impact and authenticity of images captured in the full chaos of propaganda, restoring all the real tension of the clash.
