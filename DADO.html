UNHARTED - STORIES OF A LOST FRIEND  /  2026

UnHearted: Stories of a lost frend is an elegant visual-literary work born from the intimate, searing texts of Davide Palumbo (aka DADO) and the visual identity and illustrations created by Matteo Castelli.

The book explores the complexity of the human spirit through a precise chromatic grammar: Blue gives voice to melancholy and fragility, Gray embodies disillusionment and a stark irony, while Pink encapsulates love and the ephemeral.

The combination of these colors—in the accompanying texts and illustrations—creates a visceral synergy between narrative and graphic design. It is a unique and striking publishing project, perfect for captivating the contemporary reader eager to immerse themselves in an intimate, contemplative realm.
